> Thankful for...

![Banner graphic text with bold block letters reading "This Community" (black on blue background), "API Design Matters" (black on yellow background), Davud Biesack (vertical teal on black background), with patterned background]({{
 '/assets/img/This-Community.png' | relative_url}})

OK, to start, I'll list what I'm not really thankful for. Then I'll get
to the good stuff...

I'm not thankful for Facebook. For Amazon. For Apple. For Netflix. For Google.

I'm really not thankful for X/Twitter.

<hr>

Obviously, I am thankful for Family. For Friends. For Health. For Home. _Obviously._

For having a good job at a company that I respect and that respects me.
For being surrounded by people I respect and admire and who challenge me
but also astonish me with their competence and commitment.

I'm thankful I have the opportunity to share some of my knowledge and
experience via _API Design Matters_. I'm thankful for my readers.
Your not a large group, but I seek neither volume nor fame.

And because folks don't talk about nearly enough, I want to share my
gratitude for _This API Community_ that we are all a part of.

I started working with APIs almost 15 years ago, a novice in a field
that was somewhat new to me.

It could have been daunting, and I could have failed for any number of
reasons.... but over and over again, _This Community_ was here to
welcome me, to support my curious and very opnionated ways when I worked
up the courage to even ask a question at an early _API Strategy and
Practice_ conference. Folks encouraged me to continue participating -
drafting an abstract to speak there and the new _API
Specifications Conference_, even inviting me to help the program
committee, reviewing submissions. I was never spurned by anyone when I
worked up even more courage to join a conversation in the hallway or
reception... I remember inviting myself along to a group heading to
dinner, and they made me feel like I fit right in.

I've been invited to speak on a number of podcasts and similar media
(and I'm thankful for those opportunities), and I always try to get in a
few words about my gratitude for _This Community_. I strongly
encourage others to join, to become active, to share something new or
exciting, to build up the courage to submit an abstract... because _This
Community_ will be there to support them, too. We _all_ will.

I don't wish to name people, because, well, you know who you are
(or you know who they are if you are part of _This Community_.)

So I'll just finish with...

* If you appreciate reading _API Design
Matters_, consider sharing ADM with others
* Consider submitting an
abstract for apidays or another API conference
* Consider starting
your own forum to write (but please [skip Substack](https://leavesubstack.com/)
if you are considering it)
* I even welcome guest posts here if you
have something to share! Join _This Community_ more actively. Lurking is
OK, but it's better when we collaborate. [Contact me](https://davidbiesack.github.io/contact-me).
